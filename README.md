# 🎯 GoalTracker

**GoalTracker** is a modern Android application designed to help users set, track, and manage their daily goals in a simple and visually appealing way.

The app allows users to **create goals manually, track their progress through a calendar, and view analytics** to understand their consistency and performance over time.

Built specifically for Android using **Kotlin**, GoalTracker focuses on a clean interface, simplicity, and an enjoyable user experience.

---

## ✨ Features

* 🎯 **Create Custom Goals**

  * Add your own goals manually.
  * Create goals according to your personal routine and needs.

* 📅 **Calendar Tracking**

  * Track your goals day by day.
  * Mark goals as completed or incomplete.
  * Easily view your progress across different dates.

* 📊 **Analytics & Progress**

  * Analyze your goal completion.
  * Understand your consistency.
  * Get a better overview of your daily progress.

* 🎨 **Beautiful & Modern UI**

  * Clean and intuitive interface.
  * Designed specifically for Android users.
  * Simple navigation and easy-to-use interactions.

* 📱 **Android Focused**

  * Developed specifically for Android.
  * Built using Kotlin.

## 🚀 How It Works

1. **Create a Goal**
   Add a goal that you want to track.

2. **Track Daily Progress**
   Open the calendar and mark your goal as completed when you achieve it.

3. **Build Consistency**
   Continue tracking your goals every day.

4. **Check Analytics**
   Use the analytics section to understand your progress and consistency.

## 🎯 Project Goal

The main purpose of GoalTracker is to make goal tracking **simple, visual, and motivating**.

Instead of keeping goals in your mind or writing them down somewhere, users can keep everything organized in one place and see their progress over time.

---

## 💡 Why GoalTracker?

GoalTracker is built around a simple idea:

> **Small daily actions lead to bigger achievements.**

By tracking goals consistently, users can identify their habits, monitor their progress, and stay motivated to improve.
